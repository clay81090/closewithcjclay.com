# 🎙️️ ULTIMATE MASTER ONE-CALL CLOSE

Live Co-Pilot Screen Format: HTSA Blended Framework (Challenger + JOLT + Will Hemmert Execution)

## 🛑 **THE 5 CARDINAL LAWS OF THIS SCRIPT**

1. **Never lead WITH HTSA—lead TO it.** Do not mention HTSA, Chad, or coaches in the first 8 minutes. Discovery belongs 100% to the prospect.
2. **Beat 0 is non-negotiable.** Never quote a number or run income math before getting their current income, required added income, and target number. Numbers always scale **DOWN to reality**, never up to fantasy.
3. **The Demo is interactive, NOT a monologue.** Explain one piece \\(\rightarrow\\) Show/Involve them \\(\rightarrow\\) Ask a substantive question. If you speak for 90 seconds without prospect participation, you are losing the deal.
4. **Run Chad’s "Choice of 2 Positives" before money.** Read their confidence level. If the response is flat or hedged, **do not proceed to price**—an unaddressed objection is hiding underneath.
5. **Say the price, make your recommendation, and SHUT UP.** Silence after the ask is where the close happens. Every word spoken after the number is an unearned discount.

# 🎬 **PHASE 1: OPENING, FRAME & ACCESS GATES (3 MIN)**

### 1.1 Warm Opening

*"Hey Name, CJ here with High Ticket Sales Academy. Can you hear and see me clearly?"(1 shared rapport question max—e.g., location/background. Cap at 60 seconds)."Before we dive in, are you still good on time for about 45 minutes, or are we working with a tight schedule today?"*

### 1.2 Pre-Framing & Authority (Disqualification Frame)

*"Cool. Here’s the plan for our call today: First, we’ll get completely clear on where you’re at right now, what you’re trying to build, and the options you’re evaluating.Then, if there’s a genuine fit, I’ll show you how our training, certification, and placement support address the exact gaps you have.I turn away more people from this academy than I accept. If I feel like remote sales or HTSA isn't the right move for you, I’ll tell you straight and point you in a better direction.But if it IS a strong fit and everything makes complete sense, the goal is to **get you started at the end of this call** so you’re not drifting on this for another 6 months. Does that sound fair?"(Wait for explicit "Yes". This is Micro-Commitment #1).*

### 1.3 The Upfront Access & Capacity Gates (Pre-Handling Objections)

**Stakeholder Gate**: *"One quick thing before we jump into your background—with a transition of this size, is there anyone else, like a spouse or business partner, who makes decisions with you? I ask now because I’d rather have them on with us than have you try to explain this from memory later."(If spouse exists: Note it for Phase 8).*

**Capacity Gate**: *"And time-wise—to pass certification, it requires about 10 to 12 hours a week of focused practice. Realistically, can you carve out those 10–12 hours, or is your schedule completely maxed out?"(Lock this number in).*

# 🔍 **PHASE 2: DISCOVERY – HELL ISLAND & BEAT 0 (8–10 MIN)**

### 2.1 Current Role & Breaking Comfort

*"Just so I’m not guessing, bring me up to speed on where you’re at right now. What are you doing for work currently?""What do you actually LIKE about it before we talk about what's broken?""On the flip side... what do you NOT like about it that has you looking for a change?"*

### 2.2 State-Shifting Pain Discovery (Uncomfortable Questions)

- **If they say "It's fine / comfortable":**
- *"You said it’s fine. But let me ask you straight—have you become **comfortable being unfulfilled**? Because 'fine' usually means settling."*
- **If they say "It's stable / safe":**
- *"Is it really safe... or is it just a **predictable prison with a decent paycheck**? How safe is it if you’re one company restructure away from zero?"*
- **If they say "Can't complain":**
- *"Can’t complain... or have you just gone numb to what you're missing because feeling it every day was too painful?"*
- **The Personal Cost Drill**:
- *"Real talk... what is this current situation doing to you **personally**? Stress, confidence, family time, how you feel waking up on Monday?"*[STOP & SILENCE. LET THEM FEEL IT. DO NOT RESCUE].

### 🔴 **2.3 BEAT 0: THE HARD GATE (NO PRICE WITHOUT THIS)**

1. *"Where are you at monthly right now, roughly? Ballpark is fine."*
2. *"What would a remote role need to ADD on top, monthly, for you to look back in a year and say this was a home run?"*
3. *"What’s the dream number where life looks completely different for your family?"*

**The Recap & De-Escalation**:*"So you're around **Current**, you need this to add **Target** a month to be worth it, and **Dream** is the dream. Right?For context—our established closers average between **\$10,000 and \$15,000 a month**. But in month one on a new offer, you're ramping at **\$3,000 to \$5,000** while building your pipeline. Does that realistic ramp fit your expectations?"*

### 💡 **BACK-UP QUESTION BANK (DISCOVERY SHORT-ANSWER RECOVERY)**

- *If they give 1-word answers ("More money"):* **"More money for what specifically? What is currently getting postponed because that money isn't there?"**
- *If they say ("Freedom"):* **"Freedom from what—location, fixed hours, physical labor, or an income ceiling?"**
- *If they say ("Burned out"):* **"What specific part of the week wears you down the most? How long have you been carrying that?"**
- *If they say ("I don't know"):* **"That's okay. What happened in the last 30 to 60 days that made you finally book this call today?"**

# 🏔️ **PHASE 3: HEAVEN ISLAND, CALLING & GAP RECAP (5 MIN)**

### 3.1 Goal \\(\rightarrow\\) Calling & Identity Shift

*"Switching gears... when you look at remote high-ticket sales, what’s attracting you to it—earning upside, or time freedom?"(If they focus only on money):"I love that number. But this isn't just about money. What's been **calling** you that you've been ignoring while stuck in your current role?"*

### 3.2 Future Pacing (12 Months Out)

*"Fast forward 12 months. You're plugged into a strong remote offer, earning **Their Target** month after month. Walk me through a normal Tuesday in that life. Where are you waking up? What are you doing? Who are you with?"*

### 3.3 The Gap & The 2026 Tie-Down (Cost of Drifting)

*"Let me reflect this back to make sure I get it:Right now you’re at **Current Situation**, making **Current Income**, dealing with **Pains**.Where you want to be is **Desired Life**, making **Target Income**, with **Freedom Vision**.If nothing changes over the next 12 months and we have this exact same conversation a year from now... what will staying stuck have cost you financially, mentally, and for your family?"*[SILENCE. LET THEM ANSWER].

# 🔒 **PHASE 4: PRE-CLOSE COMMITMENT TEST (2 MIN)**

*"Everything you're telling me tells me you'd be a great fit for this—**Insert Specific Reason From Discovery**.Before I walk you through how the academy works... let me ask you directly:If we go through all the details and you feel like this is a 10/10 fit for you... is there **anything you can think of that would stop you from starting today**?"*[PAUSE & LISTEN].

- **If they say Money:** *"Yeah, when it comes to structuring the investment, we have multiple ways to make that work. Besides money, if everything else aligns, is there anything else holding you back?"*
- **If they name a Spouse:** *"Got it. Is your spouse nearby right now? I'd rather answer their questions live than have you defend this from memory later."*
