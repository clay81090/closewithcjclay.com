O

Outcomes — Start with what you WANT, not what you fear

Type each outcome and press Add — it fills the next slot automatically. Drag chips to reorder. Write the real why beside each one. Add as many as you need.

O

Options — You need at least 3 for a real choice

1 option = no choice. 2 = dilemma. 3+ = a real decision. Option 1 is always "stay the course." Be specific and honest with each path.

C

Consequences — All 3 options, side by side

Be specific — not "more money" but "$150k lets me take my family to Spain." Not "hard" but "30–60 day ramp, $1,750/mo for 4 months." Circle the winner. Cross off the losers.

Green row = your winner Faded = eliminated

Tip: one bullet per line. Be specific — name the dollars, the days, the dream.

E

Evaluate — Does each option actually deliver what you said you want?

For each outcome you listed, mark whether each option delivers it. Pick the realistic timeline. The winner should be obvious — if it's not, you haven't been completely honest yet.

M

Mitigate — Neutralize the obstacles, build your game plan

You've already circled your winner and crossed off the losers. Now take every downside of your winning path and build a specific solution for each one. Reduce the downside, keep the upside.

R

Resolve & Commit — Circle your answer. Then go.

Decide · Commit · Resolve. After this, there is no "maybe." Massive action is the cure-all.
