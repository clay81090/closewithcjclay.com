OOECWR
framework

On-call discovery map. Move through Outcome → Obstacle → Evidence → Consequence → Want → Result. Capture their words. Use their language in the close.

OOutcome OObstacle EEvidence CConsequence WWant RResult
How to use: Ask, then shut up. Write their exact words in the notes. Do not pitch inside discovery. Bridge to the offer only after Result is clear.
Step 1 O

Outcome

What do they want? Ideal income, lifestyle, role, freedom. Get the destination in their words.

  • If this call went perfectly, what would change for you?
  • What does the ideal role look like day to day?
  • What monthly income would actually feel like winning?
Step 2 O

Obstacle

What is in the way right now? Job, skill gap, no network, money pressure, partner buy-in, timing.

  • What has stopped you from already being there?
  • What have you already tried that did not work?
  • What feels hardest about making a change right now?
Step 3 E

Evidence

Make the problem real. Recent examples, hours, income, stress, missed opportunities.

  • Give me a real example from the last 30 to 60 days.
  • What does that look like in hours, money, or stress at home?
  • Is this getting better, the same, or worse over time?
Step 4 C

Consequence

Cost of staying the same. Soft delivery. Let them say it out loud.

  • If nothing changes for six months, where does this go?
  • What does staying here cost you personally?
  • Who else feels this if it stays the same?
Step 5 W

Want

Why this, why now. Emotional driver under the income goal.

  • Why is this important to you right now?
  • What becomes possible if this works?
  • On a scale of 1 to 10, how ready are you to move if the fit is right?
Step 6 R

Result

Lock the vision and the gap. Then bridge to the vehicle (HTSA), not more discovery.

  • So the result you want is [their words]. Did I get that right?
  • What is missing is not ability. It is the vehicle. Fair?
  • If we can close that gap with training and placement, are you open to looking at how that works?
