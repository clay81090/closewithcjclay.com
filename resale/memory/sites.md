# Site memory

The app reads this file before it prices an item. Check the category's "First" sites before anywhere else. Do not treat a "Weak" site as a sold price.

When a real comp is saved, the app may append one line under that category: site, what it proved, and the date. Do not append sites that did not help.

## Baseball cards

- First: SportsCardsPro, then Trading Card Database. Sold comps on eBay for the same year, set, card number, and parallel.
- Weak: Beckett asking prices, generic eBay browse pages.
- A set is one item until CJ splits a single card out. A base card is not a rare parallel.

## Football cards

- First: Trading Card Database and SportsCardsPro. eBay sold for that year, set, and number.
- Weak: Beckett marketplace asks.

## Watches

- First: read the caseback for brand, model, and movement. Then eBay sold for that exact model. WatchCharts for known Seiko and similar movements.
- Star if there is a diamond, a precious metal, or the high price is more than double the low and the high is over $75.
- An untested loose watch is not a sealed, working, boxed watch.
- Known useful pages from the October 11, 2026 pass: eBay sold listings for Campbell Soup 1982, Lionel, Seiko 5Y22-8039, and Lorus Mickey; HiBid for Lionel with tin; WatchCharts for the Seiko.

## Jewelry

- Do not price until a metal mark is visible (925, sterling, 14k, gold plate, pewter, or similar).
- Star when the mark is missing or the piece might be precious metal.
- Photograph the back, clasp, and any tiny tag.

## Coins and currency

- First: Numista to identify. eBay sold or iCollector only after the date and mint mark, or the note series, are visible.
- A folder or album is one lot until CJ splits out a key date. Star a possible key date instead of pricing the whole folder as that one coin.
- A world coin is not a U.S. silver coin. The 1969 Bahamas 25 cents is a known modest example. The 1928A $2 red seal needs grade before a tight price.

## Stamps

- Identify the Scott number, count, and gum or condition before pricing.
- eBay sold for that stamp or booklet, not a broad category page.

## Elvis decanters and whiskey memorabilia

- First: HiBid and Proxibid realized prices, then eBay sold.
- Keep these separate: empty, sealed, boxed, bottle inside the box, and a working music box.
- JustAnswer is one person's opinion, not a sale. Elvis CollectionHero is an archived listing, not today's market.
- A box with no decanter is worth much less than a complete decanter. Say so.

## Space medals and souvenir metal

- A souvenir medal is not a flown precious-metal medal.
- Numista can identify. eBay sold or iCollector for the same design.

## Toys in the box, advertising watches, lighters, pens, magazines, and anything else

- eBay sold for the same item, the same completeness, and tested or untested.
- Ruby Lane, Etsy, and Poshmark are asking prices, not sales. PicClick is only a lead. Open the real listing before counting it.
- A handwritten sticker price is not the market value.

## How to add a category

Add a new heading the first time a new kind of object is saved. Keep four lines or fewer: First, Weak, and any hard comparison rule.
