Gap Math — optional
Only when it fits. Used wrong it makes you look like you weren't listening.
✓ RUN IT WHEN
  • They're underpaid and know it
  • They said "I can't afford it right now"
  • They want to wait and save up
  • There's a real, dated bill (tuition, debt, rent)
  • They're analytical and respect numbers
✗ DO NOT RUN IT WHEN
  • They said "money isn't the issue" — you'll look deaf
  • They're comfortable and doing this for identity
  • They already said they're ready — it's redundant
  • They're grieving, just lost a job, or financially fragile — it reads as pressure
  • You already used it earlier in the call
