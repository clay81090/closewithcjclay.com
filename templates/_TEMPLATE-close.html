        The plan

        Your 30 day game plan

        This is how you do it in thirty days. It is a game plan, not an obligation. Members putting in focused daily blocks have finished in 22 to 26 days. Speed comes from reps, not from time passing.

        Days 1 to 5

        Get in and get through the modules

        Day 1
        • Access opens. Your welcome email arrives, flag it as important, and you set your password.
        • You're live inside the certification platform. 55 modules waiting for you.
        • Book your 15 minute kickoff with Mark, your Success Coach.
        • Join the Mastermind community, open 24/7. Every coaching replay lives there and 750+ members are in it. Post an introduction the day you get in.
        • Put the live calls in your calendar now: Tuesdays 12:00 PM EST and Wednesdays 5:00 PM EST. Set a one hour reminder and a ten minute reminder on both.
        Days 1 to 5
        • Work the 55 certification modules. Some people are through them in two days.
        • Target is finished by day 5. Day 7 at the outside.
        • Watch at 1.25x or 1.5x. Take notes on the objection sections, you'll use them in the sandbox next week.
        • Show up for the Tuesday and Wednesday live calls from your first week. Do not wait until you feel ready.
        Days 5 to 12

        Run your reps in the AI sandbox

        Master 5 TEDx Coaching Sales Calls in AI Roleplay
        • Unlimited AI roleplay, any hour you want it. Plan on about a week here. This is the part that actually makes you good.
        • Get your first thirty awkward calls out of the way before you ever talk to a real prospect.
        • It critiques tonality, pacing, and how deep your discovery went. Run them back to back, not one a day.
        • Load it with the scripts and objections of the niche you're targeting so your practice matches the job you want.
        • Keep attending the live calls. Bring a question from your reps.
        Days 12 to 16

        Certify, then get your materials built

        Certification
        • Live mock call with Mark, who plays a defensive prospect. Scored against a rubric. It's meant to be hard.
        • Pass it and you're a Certified High Ticket Closer. That's the credential the companies in our network recognize.
        Your placement 1-on-1
        • Sit down with Nate, our Placement Director. You set your target niches, the schedule you want, and what you'll take.
        • Nate rewrites your LinkedIn and reformats your resume into the template our placed members use.
        Days 16 to 30

        Pick a few great fits and go get one

        Introductions
        • Nate introduces you directly to companies in our partner network. That's the difference between applying and being introduced, and it's most of why our members move faster than the open market.
        Interviews
        • Pick three to five companies that genuinely fit and run those interviews inside one week. This is not throwing applications at a wall. A small number of good fits, done properly.
        • Placement usually lands somewhere between one and three weeks. Around ten days is realistic if you're not dragging your feet.
        • Get in, get the reps, start earning. You can move to a better seat later with experience behind you. The first offer is a starting line, not a life sentence.

        This is the front half compressed on purpose. Finish the modules and your reps early and you leave yourself two full weeks for placement, which is the part that pays you.

        How Splitit works

        The simple version

        • $500 per month for 12 months ($6,000 total).
        • 0% interest added by Splitit.
        • No credit pull, no new loan, no new credit account.
        • You use a Visa or Mastercard you already have.
        • That card must have the full $6,000 available at checkout.
        • Your statement charge each month is $500. The rest is a temporary hold, not a charge.

        What actually happens

        1. Today at checkout: choose Splitit under the $6,000 Paid in Full button. Splitit charges $500 and places a temporary pre-authorization hold for the remaining balance.
        2. Your statement shows: only that first $500. The hold is not a $6,000 purchase on your bill.
        3. Each month after that: another $500 posts. As each payment clears, the same amount of hold is released back into your available credit.
        4. By month 12: you have paid $6,000 in twelve steps. Cash flow stays predictable instead of one large posted balance on day one.

        Bottom line: if you have strong credit and want to preserve cash flow, Splitit spreads the same $6,000 across a year at 0% interest without a credit application.
