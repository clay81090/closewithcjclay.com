A letter for you

Una carta para ti

Before the schedules and pools — this is just me talking to you.

Antes de los horarios y las piscinas — soy yo hablándote a ti.

Cesar — I know it hurts that we are leaving Ecuador. Meli and I are going to miss you just as much. Truthfully, I do not want to leave either. These weeks with you have meant more than I can put into one sentence.

I stayed at your house for almost two months this time, and more than a month last time. Watching you — competitive, smart, focused, with the whole world at your fingertips — I kept thinking the same thing: you remind me of myself. Only child energy. That quiet fire. That feeling that something bigger is possible if someone believes in you and gives you a real runway.

So I started working on something real. Not a “maybe someday.” I have been researching swim teams in Orlando, coaches, your school calendar, English options that fit around training, and what a normal week could actually look like while you live with us. A coach still needs to see your official times and evaluate you. But this is not something I said only to make you feel better. It is a plan I genuinely want to pursue.

I will fly you to Orlando. You can stay with us in our home. We will handle your meals, transportation, training arrangements, English lessons, and the adult details. You do not need to come with money or figure this out alone. Your job is to keep training, send me your best official times, and arrive ready to work hard and enjoy being part of our family here.

And this will not be only about swimming. We want time with you. Dinners together. Relaxed nights at home. Orlando adventures. Beach days. Normal family life. After practice and dinner, your night is yours — unless there is a meet or a special team event.

You already have your visa. You already have people who love you here. The only thing missing is a date on the calendar — and that is what this page is.

This isn’t goodbye. It’s the part before you come stay with us.

Con todo mi cariño,
Tío CJ
