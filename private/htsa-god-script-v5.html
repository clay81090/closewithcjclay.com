10.0 — 🔴 TRIAGE. Two questions before you open your mouth.
Q1 — have they expressed intent?
If yes, this is not a persuasion problem and re-pitching makes it worse. Go to §9.0 and recommend. If no, you have a discovery problem, not an objection — go back, don't push forward.
Q2 — which one is it?
Every objection is one of four things wearing a costume: valuation (can't choose) · information (thinks they haven't done enough homework) · outcome fear (what if it doesn't work for me) · money (capacity vs priority). "I need to think about it" and "I need to talk to my spouse" are not objections — they're containers with one of those four inside. Open the container before you answer it.
"Sure — what's the actual thing you'd be thinking about? I ask because it's almost always one of three: the money, whether it works for you specifically, or something you haven't said out loud. Which is it?"
⚠ Worse than no
"I need to think about it" is statistically worse than being told no. Never re-pitch into it. Route it. Door-lock: finish one objection before jumping to the next. Never abandon at 60% — a half-handled objection comes back at the close with interest.
