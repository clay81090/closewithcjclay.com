The roadmap

Thirty days, four phases.

Members who put in focused daily blocks have finished this in 22 to 26 days. Thirty is the honest planning number. How fast you move is mostly a function of how many reps you run, not how much time passes.

Days 1 to 7

Get set up and start the modules

  • Day 1. Your access opens, you set your portal password, and you book your 15 minute kickoff Zoom with Mark, your Success Coach.
  • Day 1. Request access to the private Facebook Mastermind. Every live coaching replay lives there, and 680+ members are in it.
  • Days 2 to 7. Work through the core certification modules. Twelve hours of content. At 1.5x speed most people finish inside four days.
  • Tuesday, 12:00 PM EST. Your first live group coaching call with Chad.
  • Wednesday, 5:00 PM EST. Live session with Christian on pattern recognition and tonality.
Days 8 to 15

Run your reps in the AI sandbox

  • Unlimited AI roleplay, available whenever you are. The point is to get your first thirty awkward calls out of the way before you ever speak to a real prospect.
  • The AI critiques your tonality, pacing, and how deep your discovery actually went. Run them back to back.
  • Program the sandbox with the scripts and objections of the niche you're targeting, so your practice matches the job you want.
Days 16 to 22

Certify, then get your materials built

  • Live mock call with Mark, who plays a defensive prospect. This is the certification gate, scored against a rubric. It is meant to be hard.
  • Pass it and you are a Certified High Ticket Closer. That credential is what the hiring companies in our network recognize.
  • 1-on-1 with Nate, our Placement Director. You set your target niches, the schedule you want, and what you're willing to take.
  • Nate rewrites your LinkedIn and reformats your resume into the template our placed members use.
Days 23 to 30

Interviews and offers

  • Nate introduces you directly to hiring companies in our partner network. That is the difference between applying and being introduced, and it is most of why our members move faster than the open market.
  • Target is three to five interviews in a week. You'll run them using Nate's structured interview prep, not improvising.
  • You also keep applying on your own. The guarantee is built around you doing both.
