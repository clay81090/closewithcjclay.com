Days 5 to 12

Run your reps in the AI sandbox

Days 5 to 12
  • Unlimited AI roleplay, any hour you want it. Plan on about a week here. This is the part that actually makes you good.

Days 12 to 16

Certify, then get your materials built

Certification

Your placement 1-on-1
  • Sit down with Nate, our Placement Director. You set your target niches, the schedule you want, and what you'll take.
  • Nate rewrites your LinkedIn and reformats your resume into the template our placed members use.
Days 16 to 30

Pick a few great fits and go get one

Introductions
  • Nate introduces you directly to companies in our partner network. That's the difference between applying and being introduced, and it's most of why our members move faster than the open market.
Interviews
  • Pick three to five companies that genuinely fit and run those interviews inside one week. This is not throwing applications at a wall. A small number of good fits, done properly.
  • Placement usually lands somewhere between one and three weeks. Around ten days is realistic if you're not dragging your feet.
  • Get in, get the reps, start earning. You can move to a better seat later with experience behind you. The first offer is a starting line, not a life sentence.

This is the front half compressed on purpose. Finish the modules and your reps early and you leave yourself two full weeks for placement, which is the part that pays you.
